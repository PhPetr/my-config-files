# Unalloyed Archive

**In an age long lost to memory, ere the stars aligned and grace was shattered...**  
This repository is a vessel—an Unalloyed Archive—wherein lie the configuration sigils I have inscribed through countless exiles into the lands of code and shell.  

Bound within are the incantations of `.vimrc`, the tarnished rites of `.bashrc`, and the whispered runes of Neovim—each a fragment of a forgotten order, each honed upon the anvil of persistence and failure. These are no mere settings, but *tools of purpose*, etched in defiance of entropy.  

Those who would draw upon these relics—beware. For knowledge, like flame, illuminates and burns in equal measure.  

**Thus do I commit these echoes to the Git of shadow, that they may endure beyond the Erdtree’s fall.**  
**May you find your own path... should you have the will to walk it.**
